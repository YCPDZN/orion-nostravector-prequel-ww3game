CONTROLS

Keyboard & mouse — click the game to capture the mouse.
WASD move · Mouse look · Left click light attack · F heavy attack · Right click / Q block (hold) · Space roll · Shift sprint (hold) · R use stim · Tab / middle click lock on · E interact · Esc pause

Touch — drag the left side to move (push to the edge to run), drag the right side to look. Buttons: ATTACK, HEAVY, ROLL, BLOCK (hold), STIM, LOCK, and ACT when something can be used.

Gamepad — Left stick move · Right stick look · RB light · RT heavy · LB block · B/Circle roll · X/Square stim · R3 lock on · A/Cross interact · Start pause

Rolling makes you briefly untouchable. Every swing, roll and block costs stamina. Rest at Signal Beacons to restore health and stims — but every war machine you destroyed will rise again. Die, and your scrap stays where you fell.
